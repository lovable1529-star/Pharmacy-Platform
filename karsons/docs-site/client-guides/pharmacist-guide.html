Pharmacist Guide

Day-to-day use of the Karsons Pharmacy Platform

Your home screen

When you log in you land on Search. That is deliberate — nearly everything you do starts with finding a patient.

Type a name or a date of birth. You can type either; you do not need both.

  • Found them? Click their name to open their record.
  • Not there? Click New patient and complete their details with them in

front of you.

Below the search box you will see today's appointments, anyone waiting as a walk-in, and any requests needing your review.

Switching branch

Top right shows which branch you are working at. Click to switch.

Everything on screen — appointments, stock, the walk-in queue — follows your selection. Patient records do not: a patient is found from any branch, because they may well have been seen at the other one.

Running a consultation

1. Start it

From an appointment, click Start consultation. From a walk-in, find or create the patient, then choose the service.

2. Verify identity

Confirm the person in front of you is who the record says. Tick to confirm — this is recorded.

3. Review their answers

If they completed the form in advance, their answers are here. Anything needing attention is highlighted:

  • Red — a contraindication or safety concern. Do not proceed without resolving.
  • Amber — worth checking with the patient.
  • Allergy warning — the product you are about to use conflicts with a recorded

allergy. The system will not let you past this without confirming.

If they have not completed the form, click Complete on their behalf and work through it with them.

4. Answer the pharmacist questions

Questions marked for you — the fever check, for instance — appear here. The patient never saw these.

5. Record what you did

Select the product and batch. Batch number and expiry fill in automatically. Check them against the vial anyway.

Record the site and route of administration, and whether it was NHS or private.

6. Sign the declarations

Read them. They are a legal record and they appear on the GP notification.

7. Submit

The system then:

  • Reduces stock for the batch you used
  • Produces the patient's record document
  • Queues the GP notification
  • Writes everything to the permanent record

Reviewing repeat requests

Requests appear on your dashboard under Needs review, colour-coded.

Green — nothing flagged. Check it over and confirm. Nothing is supplied without your confirmation.

Amber — something needs your judgement. Click Why? to see exactly which rules triggered and what the patient answered. Approve, decline, or request more information.

Red — a safety concern. The patient has already been asked to book an appointment. Review and contact them if needed.

Reading the reasoning

Every decision shows its full working: which rules were checked, which triggered, and the values used. Nothing is hidden.

The system never decides. It sorts requests so you look at the concerning ones first. The clinical decision is always yours.

Stock

Inventory shows what you hold, by batch, at your branch.

Stock reduces automatically when you complete a consultation. You should not need to adjust it manually — if the numbers drift, that is worth investigating rather than correcting.

Expiry warnings

Batches nearing expiry appear on your dashboard. Use them first.

If a batch is recalled

Go to Inventory → Batches, find the batch, click Recall.

The system immediately lists every patient who received it and prepares a notification. Review the list, then send.

Correcting a mistake

Open the record and click Edit. Make the correction and save.

The original is kept alongside your correction, with your name and the time. This is not us doubting you — it is what makes the record defensible if it is ever questioned.

Issued prescriptions cannot be edited. If one is wrong, issue a replacement. The system links the two.

Things worth knowing

A patient can be seen at either branch. Their full history follows them. If someone had a supply at Onchan last week and turns up at Kirk Michael today, you will see it.

Nothing is ever deleted. If you cannot find something, it has been archived, not removed. Ask an administrator.

Your GPhC number is on every prescription you issue. Check it is right under Settings → My details.
