Do these first

Everything below is judgement. These two are the only items that change what is known rather than what is planned.

  1. Click through the form designer while signed in. Every editor added in Parts 4–8 is a client component behind staff auth. Route compilation proves they compile, not that they render — and that is a lot of new UI resting on the compiler alone.
  2. Run pnpm build once the other session's dev server is stopped. No production build has been run all session, because a build writes to the shared .next directory.

Drawn in the design, not built

These appear in the design canvases. They are behaviour rather than styling, so the entry points exist and the mechanics do not.

Drag to reorder questions

Behaviour

The grip handles are rendered and reordering works through the up/down controls in the question editor. Dragging itself is not implemented.

Unblocked by A drag library or a hand-rolled pointer implementation with a drop indicator.

Copy a step from another service

Behaviour

Offered on the empty-form state in the design, because the blank-page moment is where a form builder feels hardest. The entry point is drawn; the picker behind it does not exist.

Unblocked by A service-and-step picker, plus id-collision handling when the copied questions land.

Duplicate a question

New

Move and delete exist. Duplicate would be new functionality, so it was left out of a redesign rather than smuggled in.

Unblocked by Your say-so — it is small.

In the design, but the data does not exist

Both were omitted on the same principle: inventing numbers on a clinical dashboard is indefensible, and a badge that asserts something false is worse than no badge.

“Clinic open” live pill

Needs data

On the Today hero. Opening hours exist in Settings but are not read by that screen, so the pill would be asserting the clinic is open without checking.

Unblocked by Reading branch hours in getTodaySnapshot.

Sparklines and “+18% vs last Friday”

Needs data

The counters on Today have no historical series behind them — only today's figures. Any trend line would be decoration drawn over invented data.

Unblocked by A daily rollup table, or a query over the consultation history.

Waiting on a decision

Not defects. Each is a choice about what the product should do, and guessing would have been worse than asking.

The ⌘K command palette

Your call

The search box in the top bar has always been inert — it has never had a handler. It is now explicitly non-interactive and hidden from screen readers, so nobody discovers a dead control by tabbing, rather than being made to look more inviting than it is.

Unblocked by Deciding whether a palette is wanted, or removing the box.

Toasts

Your call

The design includes a toast stack. Screens currently report success and failure inline, which is not obviously worse — an inline message stays on screen beside the thing it refers to. Converting is a behaviour change across roughly fifteen files.

Unblocked by A decision that toasts are actually wanted.

Loading skeletons

Your call

The tokens, the .bg-skel utility and the shimmer keyframe are all in place. Wiring them needs a loading.tsx per route — new files that change perceived behaviour.

Unblocked by Your say-so. It is now a small job.

Book / Start consultation from a patient record

Your call

Both appear in the patient-record design. Booking would need the booking screen to accept a pre-selected patient; there is no route at all that starts a consultation from a patient — they begin from an arrived appointment.

Unblocked by Two new flows, not two buttons.

FormStep.unlockedBy — the verification gate

Unused

The runtime implements it: a step stays locked until named earlier fields are answered. No seeded form uses it and the designer has no editor for it. A feature that exists and is unreachable.

Unblocked by Deciding whether the gate is wanted; the editor is then straightforward.

Payments are a deliberate fake

Known

An honest demo provider, built to your instruction. Real integration was deferred pending NMS and GPhC standards. The GLP-1 flow is payment-gated by design — the prescription is only issued once a webhook confirms Paid.

Unblocked by Choosing a provider, and the standards decision behind it.

What this audit did not cover

The audit examined the form schema surface, the designer's coverage of it, field-type reachability and link integrity. Every other domain was only compile-checked. Nothing below has been reviewed for correctness, and an absence of findings here means an absence of looking:

  • The clinical rules and triage engine
  • Payments and the webhook gate
  • Scheduling and slot generation
  • Notifications and email delivery
  • Row Level Security policies
  • The hash-chained audit log
  • Prescription PDF generation
  • Patient matching and de-duplication
