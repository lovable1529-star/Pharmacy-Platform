Test the remote Weight Management journey

A patient goes from an online form to medicine in their hand, and comes out able to order a repeat. This walks that whole path, tells you exactly what should happen at each step, and lists what is deliberately unfinished so you do not waste time on it.

Read before you start

Some of what looks wrong is meant to be there

The Known gaps table near the end lists work that is deliberately unfinished or waiting on the client. Please read it first — it will save you reporting eight things we already know about.

The queue already contains two rows saying "Unmatched patient". Those are old test submissions from the previous version of the form, which never asked for a name. They cannot be fixed retrospectively. Anything you submit should show a real name.

Before you start

What you need

  • A staff login for the platform.
  • Any three image files on your machine — a photo of anything will do. Three uploads are required and you cannot get past that step without them.
  • An email address you can make up. Nothing is actually sent.
  • About forty minutes for the full run.

Test data that makes the rules fire

The clinical rules depend on BMI, so use these unless a step says otherwise:

FieldUseWhy
Height170 cmGives BMI 31.8 — above 30, so the patient qualifies without needing a weight-related condition.
Weight92 kg
Waist104 cmAny plausible figure.
Date of birth14/03/1979An adult, comfortably inside the age rules.
MedicineMounjaro 2.5mgThe lowest strength, so no dose-step rules interfere.

Use a different name each time you run through, or you will match an existing patient record and the results will confuse you.

How to read the steps

Steps marked safety gate are the ones that matter most. Each is a place the system must refuse to let you continue. If any of them lets you through, stop and report it immediately — that is a patient-safety problem, not a cosmetic one.

Steps marked was broken are things that were badly wrong until this week. They are worth a second look.

Part one · The patient fills in the form

Go to /f/weight-management-first. No login — this is the public front door.

1

Ask to be seen in person safety gate

The first question asks how you would like to be seen. Choose "I would rather see someone in person".

Must happen — a message appears pointing you at the separate Karsons face-to-face programme, and the button changes to "Cannot continue" and stops working. You must not be able to reach any of the clinical questions.
2

Change your mind

Choose "Continue online".

Should happen — the warning disappears and the rest of the form opens: nine steps listed across the top, and the button becomes "Next".
3

About you

Fill in name, date of birth, gender, phone, email and address.

Should happen — after a few seconds a line appears at the top: "Answers saved — you can close this and come back to it later."

Then actually test that. Copy the URL, close the tab, and reopen it. Your answers should still be there.

Also check — the date of birth is three separate boxes, not a calendar. Typing 25 in the day box should give you 25, not 02 followed by a jump to the month.
4

The transfer branch

The last question on that step asks whether you are already being treated by another clinic. Answer Yes, watch the steps along the top, then answer No and watch again.

Should happen — Yes adds a step called "Your current treatment", taking you to ten. No removes it, back to nine. Answer No to carry on with this run.
5

Measurements

Enter 170 cm and 92 kg. Then switch height to feet and inches and weight to stones and pounds.

Should happen — BMI appears underneath and reads 31.8. Switching units converts the number rather than clearing it, and the BMI does not change.
6

Medical history and habits

Answer "None of the above" and "No" throughout. Then go back and answer Yes to one of the questions that reveals a follow-up — allergies, for example.

Should happen — answering Yes reveals a detail box that is required. Changing back to No hides it, and the text you typed is discarded rather than quietly submitted.
7

Evidence was broken

Three uploads: photo ID, a photo of yourself, and something showing your weight. Attach an image to each.

Must happen — each uploads and shows the file name.

Until this week every one of these refused with "This form cannot take attachments. Please bring the document with you". Since all three are required, the form could not be submitted by anybody. If you see that message, stop and tell us.
8

Collection or delivery

On "Getting your medicine", try both options.

Should happen — collection asks which pharmacy; delivery asks for an address instead. Never both at once. Choose collection and pick a branch to continue.
9

Consent, signature and submit

Read the consent list before ticking it.

Should happen — one line reads "I understand this is a remote service…". Nothing in the consent should promise an appointment. Sign in the box and submit.
The thank-you page should say a pharmacist will review and be in touch. It must not mention an appointment.

Part two · The pharmacy reviews it

Sign in and open Repeat care from the sidebar. The page itself is titled Weight Management.

10

Find your patient

Should happen — two tabs: New patients and Repeat requests. Your patient is under New patients, by name, showing the medicine requested and how long they have waited. The tab shows how many are still to call.
Also check — there are no red/amber/green filters on the New patients tab. That is correct: no automatic triage runs on new patients, and showing three zeroes would suggest they had all been checked and passed.
11

Read what they told you

Open the patient and expand "What they told us".

Should happen — every answer, grouped by section, in the patient's own units — "92 kg" not "92000". The signature shows as Provided, not as a wall of code.
12

Approve without calling safety gate

Fill in medicine, strength, quantity and directions, write a note, and press Confirm and issue — with no call recorded.

Must happen — refused: "No verification call has been recorded. Call the patient before approving." Check afterwards that the patient is still sitting in the queue and no prescription was created.
13

Call them, but do not confirm who they are safety gate

Press Record a call, choose "Spoke to them", write something in the findings box, but leave the identity tick box empty. Save. Then try to approve again.

Must happen — still refused: "The call was completed but identity was not verified."

This is the single most important check in the document. Speaking to somebody is not the same as knowing who they are, and this is the service's only identity check.
14

Try to save a call with nothing written down

Record another call, choose "Spoke to them", and save with the findings and notes both empty.

Should happen — refused: "Write down what was said. A completed call with no record is not evidence of one."
15

Record it properly

Record another attempt, tick "I confirmed I was speaking to the patient", say how you confirmed it, and write findings.

Should happen — the panel now says verified, and both attempts remain listed with times and who made them. The failed one must not be overwritten — "we rang twice" has to stay provable.
16

Approve — but change the dose was broken

Deliberately authorise a different strength from the one the patient asked for. If they requested 2.5mg, authorise 5mg. Fill in quantity and directions too, and write a note.

Must happen — go to Prescriptions and confirm it shows your strength and your directions — not the patient's request.

Until this week the prescription was built from what the patient asked for, so a dose reduced during the call was silently ignored.

Part three · Getting the medicine out

Stay on Prescriptions.

17

Try to supply with no batch number safety gate

Press Start assembling, then try Mark ready with the batch box empty.

Must happen — the button does not work, and it tells you why: "Record the batch number before this can leave the pharmacy — it is what a recall is traced through."
18

Try a pack that expires today safety gate

Enter a batch number, and set the expiry to today's date.

Must happen — refused. A pack expiring today is not fit to hand to somebody who will use it over the next four weeks. Change the expiry to a future date and it should let you continue.
19

Check that collection and delivery do not mix

Your patient chose collection.

Should happen — you are offered Record collection and never "Mark dispatched". Run through again later choosing delivery and confirm the reverse.
20

Complete the supply

Mark it ready, then record the collection.

Should happen — a green line appears: "Repeat care is now open for this patient." That is the moment they become a repeat patient — and it happens by itself.

Part four · The loop closes

None of this existed a week ago. A patient could previously finish the whole journey and still never reach the repeat form.

21

Find their Repeat Care ID

Patients → open the patient → the repeat care panel.

Should happen — an ID shaped like RC-4H7K-M2PQ, created automatically at supply. The baseline should show the height, starting weight, medicine and strength that were actually supplied.
22

Order a repeat as the patient

Open /repeat/weight-management-repeat. Enter the ID and the patient's email — but type the ID without its dashes and in lower case, the way somebody reads it back over the telephone.

Should happen — it lets you in and opens the repeat questionnaire. Dashes, spaces and capitals must not matter.
23

Try to get in without an enrolment safety gate

Try three things: a made-up ID; the correct ID with the wrong email; and the correct email with the wrong ID.

Must happen — the same message every time, whichever half is wrong, pointing you at the new-patient form. If it ever says which half was right, somebody could sit and guess IDs until one worked.

Part five · Flu, and checking nothing else broke

The flu service was working and must still be. Any difference here is a regression.

24

Receive stock from Inventory moved

Go to Inventory and press Receive stock.

Should happen — the dialog takes a product, batch number, expiry and quantity, and stays open after saving so you can enter the next batch from the same delivery.
Also check — Settings → Stock no longer has the form, and points you at Inventory instead. This was the client's first request in the group chat.
25

Try to receive stock that has expired

Should happen — a past date is refused. But a batch expiring today must be accepted — it has not expired yet. That was previously refused wrongly.
Also try — a quantity of 0. It should be refused and point you at adjustments instead, because recording zero as a delivery makes the stock ledger claim something arrived when nothing did.
26

Give a flu vaccination end to end

Take an existing flu booking through to a completed vaccination record.

Should happen — exactly as before. Batch selection offers only in-date, un-recalled, in-stock batches; the expiry fills in automatically; stock drops by one and only one.
27

Spot-check the rest

  • Today — loads, counts look sane.
  • Patients — search by name and by date of birth.
  • Consultations — open a completed one and download its PDF.
  • Vaccinations — names shown, not "Unnamed patient".
  • Documents, Reports, Compliance — open without error.
  • Dark mode — the toggle in the header; check nothing becomes unreadable.

Reporting back

For each problem

  • The step number.
  • What you did, what you expected, what actually happened.
  • A screenshot. Worth more than any description.
  • Whether it happened once or every time.

If a safety gate lets you through when it should have stopped you, send that one on its own and immediately. Everything else can be a list at the end.

Worth recording even if nothing breaks

  • Anywhere you had to guess what a button would do.
  • Anywhere the wording sounded wrong for a pharmacy.
  • Anything that felt slow. Pages take a moment — the database is hosted in Seoul and every request crosses the planet twice. Tell us which screens were worst.
  • Anything you expected to be able to do and could not find.
