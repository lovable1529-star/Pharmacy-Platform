00

Before you start

You need a staff login and the address of the live site. Ask whoever set it up if you do not have both.

Use a normal browser window for the staff side. When a step asks you to act as a patient, open a private or incognito window — otherwise you are still signed in as staff and will not see what a patient sees.

Two things are empty on a fresh system

There are no vaccine batches and nobody is on repeat care yet. Both are normal — a real pharmacy adds them once. Section 02 sets them up, and neither of the two main test runs will work until you have done it. If a dropdown looks empty, that is almost always why.

01

How it fits together

There is one patient record. Everything else attaches to it. You will use these areas, roughly in this order.

AreaWhen you use it
SettingsOnce, to add stock, pharmacists, branches and GP surgeries.
ServicesTo build or change a questionnaire. Rarely.
AppointmentsEvery day — booking, rescheduling, marking people as arrived.
VaccinationsWhen somebody comes in for a jab.
Repeat careWhen repeat requests need a pharmacist's decision.
PrescriptionsAt the counter — dispensing and handing over.
PatientsTo look somebody up, or see their history.
InventoryWhen stock arrives, expires or is damaged.
DocumentsTo find a record that was produced earlier.
ReportsMonthly — claims, audits, how busy you have been.
ComplianceIf somebody asks who did what, and when.

07

Things it must refuse

These are the most valuable tests in the document. Each one should fail to work — if any of them succeeds, report it immediately.

§

Not bugs — please do not report these

What you will seeWhy
No emails or texts arriveSending is switched off. Messages are written down but not delivered — the email provider is not connected yet.
No money is taken at paymentPayments are simulated on purpose. The workflow is real; the card processing is not connected, pending a decision on NMS and GPhC requirements.
GP surgeries do not receive anythingSame reason. The evening send is built and scheduled but not switched on.
Flu questionnaires get no green/amber/redDeliberate. Flu has no automatic rules — a pharmacist judges suitability against the PGD. Only weight management repeat requests are triaged.
Patients cannot log inBy design. Forms need no account; access is by link, or by Repeat Care ID and email.
Pages take a second or twoThe database is currently hosted a long way away. A known, planned change — not a fault in the software.
A service called xyz appearsLeftover test data. Harmless, and can be archived in Services.

§

If something is wrong

Five lines is enough, and these five make a problem fixable rather than guessable:

Test:      FLU-11
Where:     Inventory, Onchan branch
I did:     Completed a vaccination for Test Patient One using batch TEST-001
Expected:  Stock to drop from 20 to 19
Got:       Still shows 20
Time:      30 Aug, about 14:20
The time matters

Every action is recorded with a timestamp, so a rough time lets the exact record be found. “Yesterday afternoon” is usually enough.

Most valuable thing you can do

Try to break the rules in section 07 — supply somebody who should be blocked, take out stock that is not there, dispense something not paid for. Anything that succeeds when it should not is worth more than ten cosmetic notes.
